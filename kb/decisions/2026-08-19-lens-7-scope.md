# 2026-08-19 · Lens 7 scope — what the trapping lens will and will not model

> Not an experiment log. The `docs/02 §9` format assumes a recommendation that
> was actually run; there is no gate output here, only a boundary. This entry
> exists so that later readers — human or agent — stop re-proposing these four
> items as unfinished work.

## Context

Lens 7 (`trapping/`, G14) is implemented: ray-optics regime check, TIR-clipped
effective NA, power splitting across simultaneous traps, radial stiffness κ,
trap depth U/kT, and the G14 sampling requirement `f_s ≥ 10·f_c`. A review on
2026-08-19 enumerated seven candidate improvements. Four are closed here as
scope decisions; the rest remain open work.

## Decisions

### 1. Local heating at 1064 nm — will not implement

Water absorbs at 1064 nm, so the trap heats its own neighbourhood; viscosity
falls, D rises, and in microrheology that lands on the measured quantity
itself. No heating check will be added to `trapping/`.

This keeps the risk **named rather than gated** — the same treatment vibration
and stage repeatability already get. It is recorded in [`docs/06`](../../docs/06-pitfalls.md)
D6, in [`docs/01`](../../docs/01-architecture.md) §7's ungated list, and in the
README's status paragraph. Lens 5 does not cover it either (visible excitation
light only), so no other lens should be read as absorbing it.

Consequence to hold onto: a trapping verdict says nothing about whether the
medium near the trap is still at the temperature `TrapSetup.temperature_k`
claims. For a diffusion measurement taken with the trap on, that assumption is
the experiment's, not the gate's.

### 2. Faxén near-wall drag — will not correct by formula

`trapping.dynamics.corner_frequency_hz` uses the unbounded-medium Stokes drag
`γ₀ = 6πηa`. Held near the coverslip a bead feels more, by the parallel-to-wall
Faxén factor `γ/γ₀ = 1/(1 − 9a/(16h))` — +12.7% for a 4 µm bead at h = 10 µm,
which is roughly where G17 pins an oil objective on an aqueous sample. It grows
with bead radius, so a bigger probe pays more.

No correction term will be added. The route instead is **in-situ
power-spectrum calibration at the actual working height**: a measured corner
frequency returns κ and the wall-corrected γ *together*, absorbing the bias by
measurement rather than modelling it. G14 already requires that calibration, so
this is not extra work — but it must be redone whenever the working height
changes.

Recorded as [`docs/06`](../../docs/06-pitfalls.md) D8 with the full bias table,
and in [`oil-objective-trapping-in-water`](../expertise/oil-objective-trapping-in-water.md).

### 3. dial-% → mW calibration — deferred, not dropped

Measuring the trap laser's dial-to-watts curve with a power meter is deferred
under the same 2026-08-19 decision that defers *all* laser power measurement
(see [`docs/07`](../../docs/07-roadmap.md) Phase 0 for the illumination side).

Until it lands, `LaserCalibration.points` stays empty, `measured` is False, and
`trapping.gate` puts "laser dial% → mW calibration" in `assumed_inputs`. That
holds `evidence` at `assumed` and therefore `advances` at False for **every**
trapping verdict, regardless of how good the margins are. That is the intended
behaviour, not a bug to route around: force numbers computed off a placeholder
linear ramp are triage, not a trap-stiffness verdict.

What still works meanwhile: everything relative. Force *ratios*, the shape of
the force curve, which of two configurations traps harder, and the fps that
G14 demands are all unaffected by the missing absolute scale.

### 4. Non-water media — out of scope for now

The viscosity table in `trapping.dynamics` is CRC water, 0–40 °C, linearly
interpolated. Nothing else is supported and nothing else will be added
speculatively.

The code already handles this correctly and needs no change: `Medium.viscosity_pa_s`
defaults to `None`, which drops `medium.viscosity` from `available_facts` and
makes the G14 sampling check unrunnable → `BLOCKED`; and `trapping.cli check`
refuses to default a viscosity when `--n-medium` is not water's 1.33. When an
ATPS or density-matched-buffer experiment actually arrives, pass its measured
value through `--viscosity-pa-s`.

## What this leaves as actual Lens 7 work

1. **Committee wiring** — Lens 2 computes `max_fps`, Lens 7 needs it as
   `--detector-fps`, and a human currently carries it between the two CLIs.
   Phase 3 (orchestration)
2. **Axial direction ungated** — `goa.trap_force` returns `F_axial`, but all
   three hard checks and the U/kT integral are radial only
3. **No qualitative half** — lenses 4 · 5 · 6 each have a `.claude/agents/`
   subagent for what has no closed form; Lens 7 has none, though decisions 1
   and 2 above have just made the judgement calls it would own explicit

## What would reopen each decision

| Decision | Reopens if |
|---|---|
| 1 heating | A measurement whose quantity is D or η at trap powers where the heating is not obviously small, **and** an absorption coefficient for the medium exists to compute with |
| 2 Faxén | An experiment infers absolute force from a commanded velocity *without* an in-situ calibration at that height — then the bias is uncontrolled, not absorbed |
| 3 calibration | A power meter session happens; drop the measured dial%→W pairs into `LaserCalibration.points` and evidence flips to `measured` with no code change |
| 4 media | Any ATPS or non-aqueous trapping experiment. Supply a measured viscosity; do not extend the water table |
