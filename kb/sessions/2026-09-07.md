# 2026-09-07 · Microrheology attempt — abandoned, three things learned

**Intent:** microrheology in water on a 5 µm red PS bead, 100x Oil, 1×1
binned, extracting trap stiffness three independent ways (Stokes drag,
short-time diffusivity within the trap, long-time equipartition) and comparing
them.

**Outcome: abandoned without acquiring data.** No bead was ever trapped. The
session's value is in what it established instead, which is more than the
experiment would have produced.

---

## 1. What was actually accomplished

- **Light path fixed and the field seen.** Both turret shutters were **closed**,
  `CSUW1-Bright` was on `Confocal` and `FilterTurret1` on `2-Empty` — the
  reason nothing was visible. With those set (plus despeckle off) the field
  showed 5–6 isolated beads at SNR 11.4, max ≈ 30 000 ADU against a ~115 ADU
  background.
- **Objective changed to 100x Oil under software control** (`Nosepiece` state 5,
  label `6-Plan Apo LmbdD0.13 100x Oil`).
- **A working on-demand preview.** `config/session/snap_preview.py` (new):
  light on → one frame → PNG → light off. Used it to talk the user through
  refocusing by hand, which worked — the field went from soft blobs back to
  sharp cores over a handful of refreshes.
- **The user's MATLAB pipeline read, understood and committed** to
  `analysis/matlab/`, with its data contract and traps written down.
- **`trap_brightest.py` made to work on a single-camera config** and given an
  `--exclude-px` flag so a failed bead can be skipped.

## 2. The three findings worth keeping

### The beads are stuck to the coverslip — twice, unambiguously

Two catch attempts, two failures of the same kind:

| | Bead 1 | Bead 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Trap position | (−13.75, −5.48) µm | (+32.76, −7.78) µm |
| RMS excursion | 183 nm | 159 nm |
| Verdict on excursion | AMBIGUOUS (band 110–220 nm) | AMBIGUOUS |
| Implied κ | 0.24 pN/µm | 0.32 pN/µm (model: ~175) |
| Trap travel on ramp | 14.8 µm | 33.7 µm |
| Bead left at the start | 1.03 µm | 2.02 µm |
| **Verdict on ramp** | **STAYED PUT — stuck** | **STAYED PUT — stuck** |

This reproduces the 2026-09-06 survey (21 beads, median 114.9 nm against a
259 nm free expectation, most stuck) and confirms
`data/particles.yaml > abvigen-red-5um-cooh > sticking` is the dominant
practical obstacle for this probe, not a footnote.

**The excursion test cannot see it.** Both beads read "held-ish" and only moving
the trap settled it. The `--ramp-to-origin` check is therefore not optional
verification, it is *the* catch test.

Raising focus off the coverslip to look for unsettled beads was tried and did
not get as far as a catch — the new plane never came into focus before the
session ended, and enabling PFS did not help because **PFS holds a focus, it
does not find one**.

### There is still no valid px→trap-µm calibration at 1×1

`trap_from_tracking.py calibrate` at 1×1 ran and **failed its own checks**, as
it should have: anisotropy 197.5 % against a 5 % bound, bead followed 3–4 % of
a 5 µm drive, y lagging 65°. Not a bug in the fit — the bead it seeded on was
not trapped (and, in hindsight, was one of the stuck ones). The saved transform
is still the **2×2** one from 2026-09-06.

Consequence: the earlier decision to run 1×1 for localisation precision has a
prerequisite nobody had costed — a fresh calibration, which itself needs a bead
that can actually be caught. **Catch first, calibrate second, measure third.**

### The Stokes-drag stiffness has no implementation anywhere

Reading the MATLAB pipeline settled a question the experiment design had assumed
away. Of the three stiffnesses wanted:

- equipartition `kT/var` — **exists**, `estimate_kappa_from_component(...,'none',0)`
- full-PSD Oldroyd-B fit `k_fit` — **exists**, `fit_comprehensive_microrheology_v4.m`
- **Stokes drag `gamma·v/x_eq` — does not exist.** The `creepx` flow scripts
  detrend the mean displacement away (`mean_method='linear'|'moving'`) precisely
  to isolate fluctuations, so the drag signal is discarded by design.

So the "compare three κ" plan was really "compare two existing κ and write the
third". Still worth doing — (1) and (2) share their biases (localisation noise
up, motion blur down) and (3) is the independent one — but it is code to write,
not a script to run.

## 3. Operational lessons (they cost time today)

- **`pkill -f` from git-bash does not kill these processes.** A stray
  `live_loop.py` survived it, kept the camera *and* `COM10` (the CSUW1-Hub), and
  made `trap_brightest.py` fail with `Error in device "COM10"` — which reads
  like a serial fault and is not one. `taskkill //PID <pid> //F` works. Check
  `tasklist | grep python` before believing a device error.
- **Windows will refuse an atomic file swap** if any reader holds the target, so
  a preview loop that does `tmp.replace(out)` dies mid-run with
  `PermissionError [WinError 5]`. Retry with backoff; do not crash the run for a
  preview.
- **Despeckle post-processing is back on after every config load** — six `PP *
  ENABLED` entries. Every quantitative script needs its `--disable-pp` on every
  invocation, not once per session.
- `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8` is needed or any script printing `✓` dies with
  `UnicodeEncodeError` on this console.
- Repo scripts need `PYTHONPATH=/c/agentic_microscope`; the venv is
  `C:\Users\Takatori lab\venvs\auto_microscope\`.
- Windows paths in git-bash: `--out D:\data\preview` arrives as `D:datapreview`.
  Quote them.

## 4. What was decided

- The real-time tracking GUI became an explicit goal, scoped in
  [`2026-09-07-realtime-tracking-gui-scope.md`](../decisions/2026-09-07-realtime-tracking-gui-scope.md).
  The headline: `live_view.py` already has the display and the
  `--publish`/`--attach` camera-ownership solution, but **statistics cannot come
  from the display stream** because it drops frames by design and MSD lag is
  indexed by row offset.
- Microrheology's standing conditions were captured in
  [`microrheology-standard-conditions.md`](../expertise/microrheology-standard-conditions.md)
  so they stop being re-elicited every session.
- Daily logs (this file) and their index now exist, per the user's request.

## 5. State left on the instrument

- **Trap and 1064 nm laser: ON.** Left deliberately (SAFETY.md §1 — never
  dropped to tidy up).
- Illumination: off. Both turret shutters: **open** (also deliberate, §8).
- Objective: 100x Oil. Binning 1×1. Exposure 20 ms. PFS: on, holding an
  out-of-focus plane.
- Camera released; no repo process holds it.
- `Trap 1` exists in the GUI at (0, 0) after the last ramp.

## 6. Next session, in order

1. Get a bead that is not stuck — fresh sample, or find the unsedimented
   population a few µm up. Judge it by the **ramp**, never the excursion.
2. Re-run `trap_from_tracking.py calibrate` at 1×1 on that bead and get a
   transform that passes its own checks.
3. Then the passive hold (`trap_from_tracking.py hold`, added today) and the
   drag run — and write the Stokes-drag κ that does not exist yet.
4. Measure the sample temperature. `dD/D = 2.74 %/K` and it is still assumed.

# 2026-09-07, later the same day · Autofocus built, and the first objective offsets

**Intent:** build an autofocusing function and measure the x/y/z offsets between
objective lenses, to the operator's sequence: go to low mag, find focus by
moving Z, record it, change objective, re-adjust.

**Outcome: autofocus works and is validated on hardware. Three parfocal
offsets measured with an error bar. The oil half is blocked on a physical
problem the software correctly diagnosed and cannot fix.**

## 1. What was built

| file | role |
|---|---|
| `hardware/focus.py` | guarded ZDrive motion, sweep, sub-step peak by parabola |
| `detection/focus_metric.py` | the focus score, extracted so there is one copy |
| `config/session/autofocus.py` | one lens: sweep, park, optionally hand to PFS |
| `config/session/measure_objective_offsets.py` | the cross-lens sequence |
| `tests/test_focus.py`, `tests/test_objective_offsets.py` | 47 tests |

This is **the first software stage motion in the repository.** SAFETY.md §2 had
said plainly that `Microscope` exposes no stage-motion API, so that file was
updated rather than left to read as still true. The guards: `allow_motion`
required, PFS must not be servoing, every sweep capped at
`min(2800-3200 window, centre + 0.4 x free working distance)`, sample approached
only upward one step at a time, every move verified by readback. Full suite
1213 passed.

## 2. The result

Fiducial: a thin-Sharpie cross on the **far (sample-side)** surface of a 170 um
coverslip, in **transmitted brightfield** (DiaLamp) -- the mark is absorbing, so
epi would measure whatever the ink happens to emit.

| objective | focus Z um | dz vs 4x | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4x | 3114.652 (mean of 2 visits) | -- | reference |
| 10x | 3071.640 | **-43.01 +/- 7.79** | measured |
| 20x | 3067.556 | **-47.10 +/- 7.79** | measured |
| 10x -> 20x | | -4.08 | **parfocal -- inside the closure** |

Recorded in `kb/calibrations/objective-offsets.yaml`. **x/y was not measured**
-- each lens was driven as a separate autofocus rather than through the
offsets script, so only z came out.

## 3. Four things learned, in order of how much they cost

### The loop closure is 7.79 um, and it is NOT the focus metric

4x measured twice, three rotations apart, disagreed by -7.792 um. That bounds
every offset above, and it is why `10x -> 20x` is reported as *parfocal* rather
than as -4.084 um. Two 4x sweeps back to back with **no** rotation agreed to
**0.73 um**, so the ~7 um enters with the retract-rotate-return cycle: stage
return, turret repeatability, or drift over the ~5 min. Not separated.
**The test that would separate them:** repeat the closure at 100x, where the
depth of field is 0.397 um rather than 4x's 13.75 um.

### Centring a high-NA sweep on a low-mag focus nearly hit the coverslip

10x and 20x are parfocal to 4 um, so it looked safe to centre the 60x sweep on
the 20x focus. It was not: the 60x focus is >100 um BELOW 20x, toward the 2959 um
plane recorded on 2026-09-03. That put the sweep ceiling ~138 um above the
plausible true focus against a 150 um working distance -- **~12 um of clearance,
with oil on the lens.** The WD guard worked exactly as written and was still
nearly not enough, because it bounds the sweep about its *centre* and the centre
was wrong by ~108 um. **Step down the ladder; re-centre downward into Z already
occupied rather than widening.**

### A flat fine pass is not a failed fine pass

The fine sweep is deliberately centred on a peak the coarse pass found and spans
a couple of depths of field, so max/median is ~1 **by construction**. Judging it
by the coarse bar threw away a good 4x pass at 1.09 and fell back to the 10 um
coarse grid with a 6.875 um refinement sitting unused. Now two constants,
`MIN_CONTRAST_COARSE = 1.2` and `MIN_CONTRAST_FINE = 1.005` -- the latter set
well below the 1.02 observed, because a threshold tuned to one measurement is
not a threshold.

### The oil is not bridging, and a flat curve is how you tell

With ink confirmed in the 60x field (35% coverage, many edges), a 190 um profile
gave peak/median **1.01** against 1.84 / 1.70 / 1.43 at 4x / 10x / 20x on the
same mark. A lens that shows the ink's shape but resolves no edge at any Z is
aberrated, not mis-focused -- the signature of an NA 1.42 oil lens with an air
gap. Oil was on the front element, but the lens rotated in and out several times
at Z ~ 0, 3 mm clear of the glass. **Needs hands, not code.**

Also: at 60x the mark was initially not in the field at all -- a 5x5 stage grid
found it at `dy = -150 um` while the centre read 0.001 dark fraction. The
parcentricity is circular: you cannot measure the offset until the mark is in
frame, and framing it needs the offset. Grid search or `--calibrate-xy`, not a
better guess.

## 4. Bugs found by running it (all fixed)

- `DiaLamp Intensity` is **0..2100, not 0..100**, and `State` is a separate
  master switch that was `0`. Setting intensity alone leaves the lamp dark, the
  curve flat, and the run reports "no peak" for a reason unrelated to focus --
  the same failure as the Aura's `State` on 2026-09-06, on another device.
- The lamp is steep and needs re-levelling per objective: 80 / 80 / 250 / 1400
  for 4x / 10x / 20x / 60x. Level 300 at 4x saturates outright, and a saturated
  frame scores LOW on Tenengrad because clipping flattens the gradient.
- `light_off` wrote Aura line names to the DiaLamp and swallowed the failures,
  which made pymmcore-plus emit ten tracebacks that buried the sweep output.
  `hasProperty` first.
- The six `PP * ENABLED` despeckle entries were ON again, as always.
- The sweep's initial move to the low end of the span is an ASCENT from a
  retracted stage (Z ~ 0 to 2800) and the guard refused it. Permitted now, with
  the ceiling -- not the step size -- as the bound that makes it safe.

## 5. State left on the instrument

- Objective **60x Oil**, `ZDrive` **retracted to ~0**, PFS off and reading
  `Out of Range`, DiaLamp and Aura both off.
- XY at (-7934.0, 5020.9) um -- parked on the ink, 150 um from where the dry
  pass ran.
- `config/micromanager/single_cam_red_noDMD_nocom10.cfg` (new): the single-cam
  red config with every CSUW1 **and COM10** line stripped. COM10 would not
  initialise all session and the CSUW1 is not in the brightfield path.
  Consequence to remember: with the CSUW1 devices gone, `CSUW1-Bright` cannot
  be read or set, so nothing verifies the disk is bypassed.

## 6. Next, in order

1. **Get the oil bridging** and re-run 60x. The flat curve is the test: it
   should go from 1.01 to well above 1.4 the moment the bridge forms.
2. Then 100x, centring **downward** from the 60x focus, never from 20x.
3. Close the loop at 100x to separate stage repeatability from the 4x depth of
   field in that 7.79 um.
4. Measure x/y through `measure_objective_offsets.py --calibrate-xy`, which is
   written and tested but has not yet been run against hardware.

# 2026-09-07, third block · The 60x hunt, and a correction

**Intent:** the operator had found 60x focus by hand and asked for the sweep to
be run on it. Read the position, sweep it, and settle whether the oil bridges.

**Outcome: no focus anywhere in 174 um of Z at 60x. The "oil is not bridging"
diagnosis from the previous block is CONFIRMED, and an argument raised against
it during this block was wrong. x/y still not measured.**

## 1. The manual focus, read before anything moved

`ZDrive = 2947.920 um`, Nosepiece state 4 (60x Oil), XY `(-8114.8, 5021.0)`,
PFS off reading `Out of Range`, Kinetix_red 1x1 20 ms.

Read with a read-only probe, because **every script here calls
`loadSystemConfiguration`, which re-initialises devices.** `Microscope.connect`
applies no Startup preset and `allow_motion` defaults False, so a config load
does not move Z -- checked before connecting, not assumed. Worth keeping: there
is no attach-to-running-core path in `hardware/microscope.py`, so there is no
way to read state without a full device load.

`Core.Focus` is **empty** in both single-cam configs (no
`Property,Core,Focus,ZDrive`). It does not block any of this because
`hardware/focus.py` addresses `Z_DEVICE = "ZDrive"` explicitly, but
`core.setPosition(z)` on the default focus device would fail.

## 2. 174 um of Z, flat

Three sweeps, combined 272 points from **2923.9 to 3097.6 um** -- which
includes the whole band where 4x/10x/20x found this same ink (3067-3115):

| Z bin | mean Tenengrad | field median ADU |
|---|---|---|
| 2924-2934 | 0.011146 | 17314 |
| 2974-2984 | 0.010856 | 16632 |
| 3024-3034 | 0.010699 | 16575 |
| 3064-3074 | 0.010704 | 16543 |
| 3094-3104 | 0.010745 | 16503 |

Range 0.0106-0.0112, +/-3%, drifting **down** with Z in step with the field
median. **The score is tracking illumination falloff, not focus.** Against a
0.207 um depth of field, 174 um of this is conclusive.

## 3. The correction

Mid-block the no-bridge diagnosis was argued to be wrong, on the grounds that
the frame shows **sharp small specks** and a lens with an air gap resolves
nothing. **That argument is void.** The fine pass held its baseline to **0.12%
over 12 um** -- no sample feature can be that Z-invariant at 0.207 um depth of
field -- so the specks are fixed pattern, on or near the sensor. Fixed-pattern
dust is sharp *whatever the objective does*, so it is not evidence about the
objective at all.

What the ink does is the real signature, and it is the one already written down:
the blurred stroke boundary is plainly in frame (so **framing is fine and Z is
not the problem either**), the absorbing ink still gives a low-frequency
intensity pattern, and no Z resolves its edge. Shows the shape, resolves no
edge, at any Z, over 174 um. **Aberrated, not mis-focused. Needs hands.**

The parfocal guess that motivated the upward hunt -- that 60x should focus the
ink near 3067-3115 like the other three lenses -- was **tested and did not
hold.** Nothing there either. Both the log's "60x focuses >100 um below 20x"
and this block's "60x focuses with the others" are unsupported; the 60x ink
focus has not been observed at all, so there is no measured 60x offset to have
an opinion about.

## 4. max/median is fragile on a flat curve

This cost real time and is a code problem, not an instrument one.

- coarse pass: max/median **1.20**, exactly the `MIN_CONTRAST_COARSE` bar, from
  a **single point** at 2929.92 whose two neighbours sit at baseline. It reads
  as 12.3 sigma only because the baseline is so quiet.
- fine pass: max/median **1.03**, again one point (2924.35), neighbours at
  baseline, 25.7 sigma.
- The fine pass swept **through** the coarse spike at 0.207 um sampling and
  found nothing there -- which is what proves both were transients.

A ratio of two order statistics cannot tell a peak from a spike. **The fix is a
shape test: require the neighbours of the argmax to be lifted too**, or fit the
parabola and reject when its width is far below the depth of field. Until then,
read the curve, never the headline ratio. `--park` acting on a 1.03 "peak"
moved the stage 23.6 um off the operator's hand-found focus (restored).

## 5. State left on the instrument

- Objective **60x Oil**, `ZDrive` **3004.060 um** (top of the last fine sweep;
  nothing parked because no peak was found). PFS off, `Out of Range`.
- XY unchanged all block at `(-8114.8, 5021.0)`. DiaLamp off, in a `finally`.
- Camera released, devices unloaded. Curves in `D:\data\focus\`.
- **ZDrive is NOT retracted.** Retract before rotating or touching the lens.

## 6. Next, in order

1. **Get the oil bridging.** Unchanged, and now bounded harder: the test is the
   flat curve going from ~1.0 to well above 1.4, and 174 um of Z has been
   eliminated as the explanation.
2. Add the peak-shape test to `hardware/focus.py` before the next sweep, so a
   transient cannot be parked on.
3. Clean the sensor-side dust, or subtract a fixed-pattern reference. It sets
   the gradient floor that the metric divides by.
4. `--calibrate-xy` still never run against hardware.

# 2026-09-07, fourth block · Fluorescent monolayer: the dry ladder lands, 40x does not

**Intent:** sample changed to ~5 um fluorescent particles coated on glass, epi
555 ex / 605 em on the Aura GREEN line instead of the DiaLamp. Re-run the
objective-offset ladder from 4x up.

**Outcome: the dry ladder (4x/10x/20x) is measured with a 0.293 um loop
closure -- 27x better than the ink pass -- and that RETIRES the open question
about the 7.792 um. 40x WI was attempted and is NOT established. x/y is still
not measured, for a reason now proven rather than suspected.**

## 1. The headline: the 7.792 um was never mechanical

| objective | focus Z um | dz vs 4x | coarse contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4x | 3069.404 (mean of 2 visits) | reference | 2.99 / 2.98 |
| 10x | 3032.633 | **-36.771 +/- 0.293** | 7.01 |
| 20x | 3029.260 | **-40.144 +/- 0.293** | 20.78 |
| 10x -> 20x | | **-3.373** (11.5x closure) | |

The header of `objective-offsets.yaml` had parked the question "is the 7.792 um
stage repeatability or 4x's 13.75 um depth of field?" behind a proposed 100x
test. It is answered without one: same 4x lens, same retract-rotate-return,
three rotations between visits, closure -0.293 um. So this stand repeats to
about 0.3 um and the old figure was measurement, not mechanics -- the ink
fine-pass contrast at 4x was 1.02, barely above noise.

Consequence: `10x -> 20x` was never parfocal. The old pass simply could not
resolve a real 3.4 um gap.

## 2. Why the fluorescent monolayer is the better fiducial

Particles cover the whole field at every magnification, so both of the ink
failures disappear at once: nothing needs framing, and there is no
magnification at which the feature is wider than the field (the Sharpie stroke
was 175-260 um against a 156 um field at 100x). Coarse contrast rose with NA --
2.99 / 7.01 / 20.78 at 4x/10x/20x, against 1.84 / 1.70 / 1.43 for the ink on
the same three lenses.

Filter path needed no change: `FilterTurret1` state 0 is `MXR00724`
LED-DA/FI/TR/Cy5/Cy7, whose band 3 is exciter 544-565, dichroic 580-611,
emitter 589-623 nm. 605 em sits dead centre. Worth recording: there is no
EM1/EM2 device in this config -- those wheels are not Ti2 devices -- so this
cube is the only emission selection on this path.

## 3. Level the light AT focus, not near it

The first 10x pass was levelled at Z=3070 and swept: 6 of 41 coarse and 15 of
17 fine points saturated, and the script said so and refused to be trusted.
3070 was 37 um off the real focus (3032.6); at focus the particles concentrate
far more light. Re-levelled at 3033, GREEN 50 -> 5, contrast came back as 7.01.

Epi runs OPPOSITE to the DiaLamp: the lamp needed 80/80/250/1400 rising with
magnification, the Aura needs 50 / 5 / 10 per-mille at 4x/10x/20x -- 4x is
the dimmest because it spreads excitation over a 3900 um field at low NA.

A saturated frame scores LOW on Tenengrad, so over-illumination inverts the
curve. This is the same failure as the DiaLamp `State` on 2026-09-06 and the
Aura `State` before it: the third time a light-level mistake has produced a
confident wrong answer.

## 4. x/y is invalid here, and this is a proof not a worry

`locate_centroid` records the BRIGHTEST COMPACT FEATURE, which is a parcentric
offset only if it is the SAME feature at every magnification. On a dense
monolayer of near-identical particles it is not:

| objective | feature from centre | radius | that lens half-field |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4x | (+1074.0, +1500.4) | 1845 um | 1950 um |
| 10x | (+604.1, +618.8) | 865 um | 780 um |
| 20x | (+12.1, -77.6) | 79 um | 388 um |

The 4x feature sits 1845 um from centre and 10x half-field is 780 um, so
10x could not have seen it. The radii collapse as the field shrinks, which
is each lens picking whatever is brightest in its own field.

**Better protocol than the script assumes:** do not chase one feature from 4x
to 100x. Measure parcentricity PAIRWISE between adjacent magnifications -- the
20x->40x shift only needs a feature inside the smaller field, and the offsets
chain. Park XY on a unique landmark (isolated particle, scratch, coating edge;
`--locate correlate` for an edge).

## 5. 40x WI: four confounds, no number

Water was applied and it IS bridging -- individual particles and chains
resolve, with defocus donuts, nothing like the 60x oil structureless smear.
But no trustworthy focus came out. Contrast came in at 1.32, 1.40 and 7.02 on
different runs against 20x's 281 on the same sample, and the peak Z moved
between runs (2990 / 2993 / 3007-climbing / 3022). Four reasons, none excluded:

- **XY moved between runs.** It went from (-8114.8, 5021.0) to (-10471.9,
  3068.1) mid-session. Over ~3 mm the substrate own height varies, so a
  parfocal offset measured across two XY positions conflates the lens with the
  sample. This is why the script holds XY still, and the discipline was lost.
- **The correction collar is unknown.** `data/objectives.yaml` records
  `correction_collar: true` for 40x-WI, catalogue cover glass 0.15-0.19 mm. A
  mis-set collar is spherical aberration, which is exactly a contrast loss with
  a still-recognisable image.
- **THE CAMERA AND EYEPIECE ARE NOT PARFOCAL** (user, this session). Focusing
  by eye and then trusting the sensor does not work. Eye-focus read Z 3038.0
  while the camera sweep peaked at 3022.3 -- about 10-16 um apart, and that
  offset has never been measured. `LightPath` sat at `4-L100` (100% to the left
  camera port) throughout.
- Out-of-focus glow confirms it independently: lit median 305 against a
  dark-frame median of 102, so the raised background is diffuse
  fluorescence, not stray light. At focus that light would be in the particles.

## 6. Two verdicts of mine that were wrong, and how

Recorded because both were produced by bad tests that looked quantitative.

- **The field is tilted by 20 um.** The quadrant test bottom-left region
  read 0.24-0.25 at every Z -- it is a dark corner with no signal, so its
  argmax was noise, and the 20 um spread was that noise. The real regions all
  agreed. *Check that every region has signal before comparing their argmaxes.*
- **The centre blobs are a bubble fixed in the optics.** The XY-shift
  correlation gave 0.1299 unshifted vs 0.1181 shifted -- both essentially
  ZERO. Smooth low-contrast blobs have nothing to correlate, so the test could
  not decide, and printing the larger of two null results as a verdict was
  wrong. *A comparison of two numbers needs a scale before it needs a winner.*
